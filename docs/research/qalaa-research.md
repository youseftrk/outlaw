## 2. UAE sovereignty & data

- **Microsoft × Core42 sovereign cloud.** On 18 Mar 2025, DGE signed Microsoft and Core42 (G42) to run a sovereign cloud handling **11+ million daily** government interactions; Peng Xiao: "Core42's Sovereign Public Cloud, powered by Azure and enhanced by our sovereign controls platform, Insight, enables government entities to maintain data sovereignty while harnessing hyperscale innovation." Source: https://www.dge.gov.ae/en/news/microsoft-g42
- **Stargate UAE.** Announced 22 May 2025: a **1GW** AI cluster (first 200MW live in 2026) inside a **5GW**, 10-sq-mile UAE–U.S. AI Campus in Abu Dhabi, built by G42 with OpenAI, Oracle, NVIDIA, Cisco and SoftBank — the largest AI campus outside the US. Sources: https://openai.com/index/introducing-stargate-uae/ ; https://www.g42.ai/resources/news/global-tech-alliance-launches-stargate-uae ; https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/stargate-uae-ai-datacenter-begin-operation-2026-2025-05-22/
- **Federal Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).** Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021, in force 2 Jan 2022: consent-based processing, extraterritorial scope, and rules governing cross-border transfer of UAE residents' data; the UAE Data Office is the federal data regulator. Sources: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws ; https://ai.gov.ae/personal-data-protection-law/
- **Dubai Data Law (Law 26 of 2015).** Establishes sharing-and-protection duties for Dubai government data, administered by Dubai Data Establishment (Digital Dubai). Referenced at https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws and the UAE AI guide: https://ai.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/AIGuide_EN_v1-online.pdf
- **Security standards.** Government entities are bound by the UAE Information Assurance framework (TDRA) and emirate-level regimes such as Dubai's ISR (Dubai Electronic Security Center); telecom IAM policy is enforced by TDRA with du/e&. Source: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws

## 5. Comparable products / moat check

Everything below governs agents **inside one organisation**. None offers bilateral cross-entity grants — "Entity A's agent requests, Entity B accepts/enforces/revokes, and both get a plain-language record" — which is Qalaa's wedge.

- **Microsoft Entra Agent ID** (GA 2025): gives agents first-class Entra identities, Conditional Access and governance for Copilot Studio/Azure AI Foundry agents — inside a Microsoft tenant; no cross-organisation grant/accept flow or sovereign deployment story. https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-entra-blog/announcing-microsoft-entra-agent-id-secure-and-manage-your-ai-agents/3827392
- **Okta / Auth0 — Auth for GenAI, Token Vault, Agent Gateway, Cross App Access (XAA)**: OAuth-anchored tokens and consent for agents calling apps/MCP servers within one enterprise; XAA is early protocol work; not an inter-government authority exchange. https://www.okta.com/newsroom/press-releases/auth0-platform-innovation/ ; https://auth0.com/ai.md
- **AWS Bedrock AgentCore Identity** (preview Jul 2025): workload identities + credential providers for agents on AWS, integrating Cognito/Okta/Entra — scoped to the AWS runtime; no sovereign cross-entity acceptance flow or auditor-readable record. https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/introducing-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-securely-deploy-and-operate-ai-agents-at-any-scale/
- **Descope Agentic Identity Hub**: short-lived scope-limited credentials and audit for agents calling your APIs/MCP servers — developer-focused, single-organisation control plane. https://docs.descope.com/agentic-identity-hub
- **Keycard**: OAuth 2.1 security-token service for human↔agent delegation chains — infrastructure/protocol layer, not a government-facing grant-and-revoke console with plain-language records. https://docs.keycard.ai/
- **Permit.io / Cerbos / Oso**: policy engines (ABAC/ReBAC/PDP) answering "is this call allowed" inside one org; they decide policy, they don't issue revocable cross-entity permissions or produce citizen-facing records. https://www.permit.io ; https://www.cerbos.dev ; https://www.osohq.com
- **Anthropic MCP authorisation**: an OAuth 2.1-based spec for client↔server auth — standardises plumbing, not governance of which entity may grant what. https://modelcontextprotocol.io
- **WorkOS / CyberArk**: enterprise auth (AuthKit) and privileged machine-identity vaulting respectively — directory/secrets focus, not cross-entity delegated authority.

## 6. The 90-second version (plain language)

1. The UAE just made a public bet: half of all government services will run on AI agents within two years.
2. Abu Dhabi is spending 13 billion dirhams to become the world's first AI-native government by 2027.
3. Dubai appointed an AI chief in every government department and wants agents everywhere in business too.
4. These agents will constantly need help from each other — police agents asking health agents for records, transport agents asking utilities.
5. Today, an AI agent from one government body has no clean way to ask another body for permission to act.
6. Qalaa is one switch for that: an agent asks, the other entity says yes, and the agent gets a time-limited permission.
7. Without the permission, the door stays shut. With it, the door opens. The moment it's taken back, it shuts again.
8. Every ask, every yes, every revoke is written down in plain language anyone can read.
9. The market for controlling AI agents is growing 20–45% a year, and Gartner says weak controls are why agent projects fail.
10. Nobody else does this between organisations. That's the gap Qalaa fills — in the country moving fastest on agentic government.
